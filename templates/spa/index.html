Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and booking process.

How do I book a spa treatment? expand_more

Booking is simple! You can click "Book Now" in the navbar, select your preferred service, choose an available therapist, pick your desired date and time, and confirm. You can proceed without logging in initially, and you will only be asked to sign in or register at the final step to secure your reservation.

What is Serenity Wallet and how does it work? expand_more

Serenity Wallet is your prepaid credit account in our app. You can top up your wallet with custom amounts, and use it to instantly pay for spa treatments and packages at checkout. It makes booking fast and secure.

How do Session Packages work? expand_more

We offer packages of 10 sessions for signature treatments like Aromatherapy Massage and Radiance Facials. Once purchased, these packages will appear in the "Your Active Packages" section on your Home dashboard. You can click "Use Package" at any time to instantly schedule a session from your remaining quota.

What is the cancellation policy? expand_more

You can cancel or reschedule your reservation up to 24 hours before your scheduled treatment time directly from your Booking History profile. Cancellations made within 24 hours of the appointment may incur a late cancellation fee.

Do I need to select a specific therapist? expand_more

No, selecting a specific therapist is optional. If you choose "No Preference", the system will automatically allocate your booking to the next available specialist for your chosen time slot.

How to Use

Learn how to easily navigate our spa booking system and packages.

1

Browse and Pick Service

Go to the Service catalog or explore our featured packages. Choose from massage treatments, rejuvenating skin therapies, body wraps, or pre-purchased bundles.

2

Select Your Therapist

View available specialists and select one that fits your treatment. Or choose "No Preference" if you want the first available professional.

3

Pick Date and Time

Our calendar dynamically displays available time slots based on your selected therapist's schedule and the duration of your treatment. Choose what fits your day.

4

Confirm and Secure

Review your details at the confirmation step. When you click confirm, you will be prompted to log in or register. Once authenticated, your booking is securely saved!

stars Using Purchased Multi-Session Packages

Purchasing a package saves you money and credits your account with 10 sessions. Here is how you schedule them:

  1. Ensure you are logged into your account.
  2. On the Home page, look at the "Your Active Packages" widget.
  3. Click "Use Package" next to the package you wish to schedule.
  4. Pick a date and time slot. Your session will be booked immediately, deducting one session from your active package balance without requiring checkout payment.
