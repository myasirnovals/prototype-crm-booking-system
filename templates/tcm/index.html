Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and booking process.

How do I book a TCM treatment or consultation? expand_more

Booking is simple! You can click "Book Consultation" in the navbar, select your preferred treatment (e.g. Laser Acupuncture, Teleconsult, or Tuina), choose an available physician, pick your desired date and time, and confirm. You can proceed without logging in as a guest, and you will only be offered optional sign-in at confirmation to track your medical records.

What is Clinic Wallet and how does it work? expand_more

Clinic Wallet is your prepaid healthcare credit balance. You can top up your wallet with custom amounts, and use it to instantly pay for TCM consultations, acupuncture sessions, and bundles at checkout. It makes booking seamless and contactless.

How do Session Packages work? expand_more

We offer structured clinical packages (e.g., 5 to 10 sessions) for intensive recovery courses like Pain Relief Acupuncture and Orthopedic Tuina. Once purchased, these bundles will appear in your "Active Packages" section. You can click "Use Package" at any time to schedule a session from your remaining quota.

What is the cancellation policy? expand_more

You can cancel or reschedule your reservation up to 24 hours before your scheduled appointment time directly from your Booking History profile. Cancellations made within 24 hours of the appointment may forfeit the 50% clinic holding deposit.

Do I need to select a specific physician? expand_more

No, selecting a specific physician is optional. If you choose "No Preference", the clinic system will automatically allocate your consultation to the licensed practitioner on duty for your chosen time slot.

How to Use

Learn how to easily navigate our TCM clinical booking system and packages.

1

Browse and Pick Service

Go to the Service catalog or explore our featured packages. Choose from massage treatments, rejuvenating skin therapies, body wraps, or pre-purchased bundles.

2

Select Your Therapist

View available specialists and select one that fits your treatment. Or choose "No Preference" if you want the first available professional.

3

Pick Date and Time

Our calendar dynamically displays available time slots based on your selected therapist's schedule and the duration of your treatment. Choose what fits your day.

4

Confirm and Secure

Review your details at the confirmation step. When you click confirm, you will be prompted to log in or register. Once authenticated, your booking is securely saved!

stars Using Purchased Multi-Session Packages

Purchasing a package saves you money and credits your account with 10 sessions. Here is how you schedule them:

  1. Ensure you are logged into your account.
  2. On the Home page, look at the "Your Active Packages" widget.
  3. Click "Use Package" next to the package you wish to schedule.
  4. Pick a date and time slot. Your session will be booked immediately, deducting one session from your active package balance without requiring checkout payment.
